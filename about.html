Who We Are

A Passion for
Lifelike Flora

Blossom Art Galleries is a premier wholesale artificial flower company supplying high-quality decorative plants and flowers to retailers, event planners, and interior designers across the United States.

Founded with a passion for beautiful, lifelike artificial flora, we source only the most realistic and durable materials. Our collection spans from delicate creepers and bold flower bunches to elegant fittings and grass mats — ensuring every space can be transformed with nature's beauty without the maintenance.
