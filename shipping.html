Shipping & Delivery

Domestic Shipping (India)

We provide nationwide shipping across India. Standard delivery typically takes 5-7 business days depending on your location. We partner with reliable logistics providers to ensure your delicate floral items are handled with care.

International Shipping

Blossom Art Galleries ships to clients worldwide. We offer both Air Freight and Sea Freight options for international wholesale orders.

  • Air Freight: Recommended for urgent or smaller wholesale orders. Transit time is typically 7-14 business days.
  • Sea Freight: The most cost-effective option for large bulk container orders. Transit times vary by destination (typically 30-45 days).

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 2-3 business days. Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email or WhatsApp to monitor your shipment's progress.

Packaging Policy

To maintain the structural integrity of our artificial and dried plants, we use double-walled reinforced boxes and specialized internal padding. For very large items or bulk shipments, wooden crating may be used for additional protection.

Need a Shipping Quote?

For bulk international orders, please contact our logistics team for a customized shipping estimate.
